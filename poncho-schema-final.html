Note for knitters
The background color of each quadrant is the only firm guide. The secondary colors and narrative descriptions are entirely optional suggestions —the weaver is free to adapt, enrich, or replace any motif according to their judgment, their tradition, and what the material allows. What is in their hands is the art.
